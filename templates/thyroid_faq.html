Thyroid Health FAQs

Learn about thyroid health, common laboratory measurements, and how to interpret AI-assisted screening responsibly.

1. What is the thyroid gland?

The thyroid is a gland in the neck that produces hormones involved in regulating metabolism, energy use, and other important body functions.

2. What is TSH?

TSH stands for thyroid-stimulating hormone. It helps regulate thyroid hormone production and is commonly measured when evaluating thyroid function.

3. What are T3 and T4?

T3 and T4 are thyroid hormones. Healthcare professionals may evaluate their levels alongside TSH and other clinical information.

4. What is hypothyroidism?

Hypothyroidism occurs when the thyroid does not produce enough thyroid hormone to meet the body's needs. Diagnosis requires appropriate clinical evaluation.

5. What is hyperthyroidism?

Hyperthyroidism occurs when the thyroid produces excessive thyroid hormones. A healthcare professional can determine whether testing or treatment is needed.

6. Is the AI prediction a medical diagnosis?

7. What should I do after receiving a prediction?

Discuss any concerning results with a qualified healthcare professional. Bring relevant laboratory reports, medical history, and your prediction report if you wish to discuss it.

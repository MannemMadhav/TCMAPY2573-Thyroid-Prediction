Thyroid Information & Education

Learn about thyroid-related screening, laboratory measurements, the two-stage prediction workflow, and how to interpret this application as an academic decision-support tool.

01

What Is the Thyroid?

The thyroid is a gland that produces hormones involved in regulating several important body processes. Thyroid function is evaluated using medical history, clinical assessment, and appropriate laboratory investigations.

This project explores machine-learning-based screening using structured thyroid-related clinical and laboratory data.

02

Thyroid-Related Measurements

TSH

Thyroid-stimulating hormone, an important measurement in thyroid evaluation.

T3

A thyroid hormone measurement that may be used during clinical evaluation.

TT4

Total thyroxine, a measurement of thyroid hormone in the blood.

T4U

A thyroid-related laboratory measurement represented in the project data.

FTI

Free thyroxine index, interpreted by clinicians alongside other findings.

TBG

Thyroxine-binding globulin, a protein involved in thyroid hormone transport.

These are educational descriptions. The actual model input features depend on the dataset and preprocessing used in this project.

Normal vs Thyroid Dysfunction

The first stage uses XGBoost to perform the project's binary screening task, separating the Normal category from the Thyroid Dysfunction category.

Calibrated Stacking Ensemble

The Stage 2 architecture combines predictions from multiple base learners using a Logistic Regression meta-learner.

The meta-learner that combines base-learner outputs.

  4. Download the prediction report if needed.
  5. Discuss health concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.
